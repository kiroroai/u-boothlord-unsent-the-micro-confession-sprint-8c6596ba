Entry for make.fun's [Unsent: The Micro-Confession Sprint](https://make.fun/bounty/u-boothlord-unsent-the-micro-confession-sprint-8c6596ba): one original two-part micro-confession — a `To:` line naming who it was meant for, and the unsent line itself, exactly as typed.

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, deterministic, no network) writes the confession to `/output/entry.txt`:

> To: Kak Ardi
> i broke your guitar back in 2016 and blamed the cat. i've practiced saying sorry a hundred times and i still can't say it to your face.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
